Smart vs plain

The smart plan buys more when bitcoin is cheap against what holders paid: 2× the amount under the realized price, 1× to twice it, ½× to three times, ¼× above. The plain plan buys the amount each time.

Each buy is at that day's closing price (UTC), with no fees; the last day is at the latest price. A monthly plan buys on its first day's date each month, or a shorter month's last day. The smart plan buys the amount times 2, 1, ½ or ¼ by that day's MVRV, the price over the realized price (what every coin last moved at, on average): under 1, under 2, under 3, or above; 1× before MVRV begins. That rule was picked up front, not fitted to the past, but any look back is hindsight. Prices and MVRV are this server's daily series: prices from their first day, MVRV from its first.
