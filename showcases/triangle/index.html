How it's made

From the UTXO set at the end of every week since 2009, each output counted at the block that made it: where its coins last moved. The blocks are cut into 1,200 equal stretches, about 800 each; a point is what's left, in BTC, of one stretch at one week's end. Both axes are blocks, so the edge, where blocks are made, is the diagonal; the marks on it, ◆, are the halvings.

A stretch's most is its largest at a week's end, so coins made and spent within one week never show. The table's years are exact, block for block.
