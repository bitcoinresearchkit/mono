      How it reads

      1. The long-term cloud: how holders feel

        The median price paid by coins held over 4 months, over 5 months and over 6 months: three lines close together, the cloud between them.

        Price above it, more than half of the dollars each of those groups put in is in profit; below it, more than half is at a loss; inside, the groups differ. That's the compass's north and south.

      2. The short-term cloud: how recent buyers feel

        The same for coins held under 4 months, under 5 months and under 6 months: what recent buyers paid.

        Above it, more than half of what each group paid is in profit; below it, more than half is at a loss; inside, the groups differ. That's east and west. Price is each day's close.

      3. Three trends set the territory

        For the same three short-term groups, a line on the lowest price any of their coins was bought at, a support, until price touches it; then on the highest, a resistance, until price touches that.

        When all three hold supports, it's bull territory; all three resistances, bear; split, limbo.

      A median here is by dollars and counts mostly the coins that move (each age group weighted by how likely its coins are ever to be spent), so lost coins don't hold a cloud down. Every state is read from that day's numbers alone, never from what came after: today's can change by tonight's close.
