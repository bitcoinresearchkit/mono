Where the data comes from

Bitview's own code computes it from Bitview's own Bitcoin node: the blocks, and the pending transactions in its mempool. Some of the data comes from elsewhere:

  • Before August 2010 (block 74,641), there is no price, and it reads zero. From then until January 5, 2015, the price comes from an older record of exchange prices, whose original source isn't documented; by its end it followed Bitstamp. From January 5, 2015, it comes from Bitfinex's minute closing prices. From block 340,000 (later in January 2015) on, it's estimated from the chain itself, not read from an exchange.
  • Mining pools are recognized with the public list of pools kept by the mempool.space project, and their logos are copied from mempool.space.
