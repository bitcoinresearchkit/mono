Bitview on this server

Every Bitcoin block, read and measured by this server: its series, to chart, explore and fetch.

Apps

Studio→

Chart any series: overlay, stack, derive with formulas, then keep, share or export.

Series→

Every series, folder by folder: what it is, its history, and how to fetch it.

Smart DCA→

Bitcoin on a schedule, more when it's cheap: any plan since 2010, beside the plain one.

Globe→

Where blocks are mined, by their pools' home countries, live and since 2011.

Calendar→

Every day's leading pool, and every block of any day.

The Satoshi Trail→

Supply by the price it last moved at, through time, in two or three dimensions.
